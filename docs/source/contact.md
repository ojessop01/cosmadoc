# Contact and getting help

## Before you ask

Many questions are answered in these pages.  Try the search box at the top
left, and these pages in particular:

- [Your first job](firstjob.md) and [job problems](jobproblems.md), for jobs that
  fail or wait;
- [SSH access](ssh.md#cannot-get-access), if you cannot log in;
- the [FAQ](faq.md).

## COSMA support

Email **cosma-support@durham.ac.uk** for technical questions about COSMA:
problems with logging in, jobs, software, modules or storage, and requests for
software to be installed.

A clear request gets a quicker answer.  Please include:

- your **COSMA username**;
- the **login node** you were using (`hostname`), or the **job ID**;
- the **exact command** you ran, and the **directory** you ran it in;
- the **exact error message**, copied as text rather than a screenshot;
- **when** it happened;
- what you have **already tried**.

For a job, the job ID, its state (`sacct -j JOBID`), and the relevant lines
from its output and error files are usually the most useful information.
Please do not send passwords or private SSH keys.

## SAFE support

For problems with the [SAFE](https://safe.epcc.ed.ac.uk/dirac/) website
itself, such as being unable to log in to SAFE, contact SAFE support at
dirac-support@epcc.ed.ac.uk.

## Feedback

If you find something in these pages that is wrong, out of date or unclear,
please tell cosma-support.  We are also happy to hear about your
achievements with COSMA, such as papers, results and images, which we can help
to publicise.

## Visiting us

You are welcome to drop in to our offices, or to ask for a call.  Current room
locations are at the bottom of the
[ICC staff information pages](https://icc.dur.ac.uk/index.php?content=Staff/Staff).
